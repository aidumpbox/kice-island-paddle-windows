Kice Island Paddle Windows

Best 2-hour stretches to cross without fighting the current
Current runs weakest near slack water — the hour on either side of a high or low tide — and strongest at mid-tide. Windows below are centered on slack water and trimmed to daylight. High-tide windows also give you more water over the sandbars near Kice, so they're marked best when both apply.
