NOAA Station 8724967 · Caxambas Pass

Kice Island Paddle Windows

Calm-current stretches to cross, sized from predicted tide physics and checked against wind, storms, and marine advisories
Each window is centered on slack water and its width comes from how fast NOAA's predicted tide curve is rising or falling near that slack — steep curves mean current builds fast and get a tighter window, flat curves get a wider one. Wind speed, wind direction relative to the current, storm risk, and any active Coast Guard/NWS marine advisory are all layered on top. Current isn't directly measured at this pass (no NOAA current station nearby) — window sizing is a physically-motivated estimate from tide height data, not a measurement. Treat tight windows and "brisk" days with extra caution, and calibrate your own judgment against conditions you actually observe on the water.
